# A more playful look: slice 1

How slice 1 of issue #9, "tokens and shape", was built. The plan is in [`plan.md`](./plan.md) and the design in [`design.md`](./design.md); [`README.md`](./README.md) lists the folder's files. The rules that came out of it are in [`rules/look.md`](../../rules/look.md).

## Tokens and shape (built 2026-10-16)

Nothing on screen changes color yet: the feature tokens exist, and the tiles, headings and empty states use them in slice 2. What changes is the shape: rounder corners, cards with an edge instead of a shadow, and the browser's own surfaces in the theme's colors.

- **The feature tokens** (`assets/css/theme.css`): `--feature-<name>`, `-tint` and `-strong` for `todos`, `shopping`, `meals`, `recipes`, `vacations`, `whiteboard` and `household` (the household and the kids share mauve, one token). The names are the dashboard tiles' keys, so slice 2's `features.ts` can map a tile to its tokens without a second set of names.
- **The tints are calculated per color**, as the most that keeps the text, the muted text, a hovered flat button and the red error text at 4.5:1 or more on the page and on a surface, up to 6% in light mode and 8% in dark mode (the Tonight tile's, which stays the strongest). Red is the pair that sets the limit everywhere. Light: blue 5%, mauve 4%, the others 6%. Dark: blue, peach and mauve 8%, the others 7%. The design's "tint behind muted text on a card stays at 8%" holds.
- **The strong shades are darker than the plan's figures** (user decision during the slice): green 11%, peach 22%, yellow 31% and pink 30% of Latte's text, where the plan had 5, 16, 26 and 27%. The plan's figures reach 3:1 on the page (3.05 to 3.08), but on the feature's own tint in light mode green, peach, yellow and pink fell to 2.83 to 2.88, so the Tonight tile's peach icon, on peach, would not have held the "3:1, so it would hold even if it carried meaning" of the design. The new figures are the least that reaches 3:1 there, plus one point of margin: 3.03 to 3.05 on the tint, 3.19 to 3.23 on the page. Blue, teal and mauve needed nothing (teal 3.10, blue 4.07, mauve 4.53 on their tints).
- **The fallback for an older browser is named at each use** (D3 of the eng review). One custom property can't hold two fallbacks, the outline color for an edge and the text color for an icon, so the strong shades are declared only inside `@supports (color: color-mix(in oklab, red, red))`, and every use writes `var(--feature-<name>-strong, <fallback>)`. All seven are in the block, also the three that are no mix, so that the rule has no exception.
- **The radius scale**, `--radius-field` to `--radius-dialog`, overrides Quasar's corners in `theme.css` (D4 of the eng review): outlined fields, buttons, menus, tooltips, notices, the date picker, cards and dialogs. The filled and standout fields, button groups and the time picker are not overridden, because the app uses none of them. The selectors double a class (`.q-card.q-card`) so that they win over Quasar's whichever stylesheet loads first.
- **The app's own corners** that were written as 4px follow the scale: the tile's stretched link (card) and the wordmark's link (control), the trip's link in the vacations' tile (field), the bordered lists of recipes, vacations and days on `/meals` and the vacation form's boxes (card), a proposed dinner's row, the slots of the add-dinner dialog and a day's "add" button on `/meals` (control). The first and last day on `/meals` round off with their list, so that today's tint doesn't stick out of the corners.
- **Cards have a 1px edge in `--theme-separator` and no shadow**; a card inside a dialog keeps Quasar's shadow and has no edge. Menus and notices keep theirs. A real border, not a shadow drawn as an edge: forced-colors mode drops shadows and keeps borders.
- **The browser's own surfaces**: `::selection` is the text's color on mauve at 20% (5.31:1 light, 5.82:1 dark on a surface), and the caret, the scrollbars and `accent-color` take the theme's colors.

Checked in the build: Lightning CSS keeps the `@supports` block and the `color-mix()` in it, rewrites the `light-dark()` inside it as it does elsewhere, and keeps the `:not(.q-dialog .q-card)` selector (seen in the generated `entry.*.css`).

Tests:

- `utils/theme-tokens.spec.ts` (new) reads `theme.css` and checks that every feature has its three tokens, of one hue, as `light-dark()`; that the strong shades are declared only inside the `@supports` block; that every strong shade is at 3:1 or more on the page, a surface and its own tint, and every tint keeps the four pairs above 4.5:1, in both modes; that the Tonight tint is 6% and 8%; and that selected text is above 4.5:1. The arithmetic is in `tests/support/color-contrast.ts` (the blend of a translucent color, the oklab mix and the WCAG ratio); the spec checks it against figures computed when the theme was built. Verified to fail with the Tonight tint at 8% in light mode and with peach at the plan's 16%.
- `a11y.e2e.ts` checks that the corner of a tile's highlight and of the wordmark's highlight equal `--radius-card` and `--radius-control` in pixels (D9 of the eng review); verified to fail with the tile's old 4px. The scans passed in both modes.
- Screenshots at 375px before and after, in light mode, dark mode, forced colors and with the root font at 200%: the dashboard, the to-dos, the shopping list, `/meals`, the recipes, the vacations, the kids and the whiteboard, and the account menu. In forced colors the cards' edges are drawn in the system's color, so the tiles stand apart without the shadow.

Noticed, not changed:

- **At 200% text the tiles' headings run past the tile** ("Whiteboard", "Vacations"): they don't wrap. The same before the slice; the edge only makes it visible. The tiles' `min-height` doesn't cut anything off.
- The whiteboard's board is still drawn with an edge in `--theme-outline`; the neutral `--theme-separator` edge of the design is slice 2's.
